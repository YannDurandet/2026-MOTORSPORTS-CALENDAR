### How it was matched
- **One FIA row is one layout, not one venue.** The FIA lists Bahrain five times and Motorland Aragón fourteen. Rows are matched to a track by venue, then to a layout by the SVG we draw and its length.
- **`fia` in `tracks.json`** holds `{ "grade", "pole" }` and feeds the grade and pole cells on `/tracks/[slug]`. A single-layout track carries it on the track; a track with `layouts` carries it on each layout the FIA lists (the page swaps it with the layout buttons). `pole` is left out when the FIA gives none. `npm run check` validates both.
- **A track only gets `fia` when the FIA lists a layout we show.** Suzuka (East Course only), Homestead (Formula E layout only), Circuit of the Americas, Daytona, Atlanta and the other ovals, Circuit de la Sarthe, Shanghai's F1 layout and the rest of D have none, so their pages show no licence cell.
- **Different names, same circuit:** Darwin = Hidden Valley, Perth = Barbagallo (Wanneroo Raceway), Gold Coast = Surfers Paradise, Taupo = Bruce McLaren Motorsport Park, Portimão = Algarve, Barcelona = Catalunya, Bathurst = Mount Panorama, Mosport = Canadian Tyre Motorsport Park, Madrid = Jarama, Lusail = Qatar, Las Vegas Strip = Las Vegas Grand Prix, Mexico City = Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez, Yas Marina = Abu Dhabi.
- **Licence expiry dates are not stored.** The list is a March snapshot: several licences it shows (Baku, Monaco, Spa, Zandvoort…) have expired since and will have been renewed.
- **Per-layout specs.** Length, turns and lap record are stored on each layout, not on the track (`layouts[].length`, `.turns`, `.lapRecord`; `null` when no reliable figure exists, and the page hides that cell). Lengths come from this list where it has the layout, otherwise from Wikipedia's circuit infobox. Where the two disagree on a layout we show, the FIA figure wins (Donington National 3.185 km, Indianapolis road course 3.91 km, Mexico FE 2.63 km), except Shanghai FE (see its row). Layouts with no figure found: Misano WSBK lap record, Miami FE turns.
- **Source typos:** Sidi Daoui's length reads "4482.9", London ExCeL FE's reads "2086" (2.086 km).
- **Nothing in C has a page, a `tracks.json` entry or an SVG.** When a venue is added, move its row out of C (`npm run gen:map-status -- --check` fails while a proposed slug in C exists in `tracks.json`).
